About

I take machine sub-systems from a client requirement to released drawings, and I use simulation to make them lighter and cheaper without losing stiffness.

For two years at Ace Designers I've designed twin spindle vertical machining centres: sizing ball screws, servo motors and LM guideways, selecting bought-out parts, and detailing drawings with GD&T (ASME Y14.5) and tolerance stack-ups.

Before that I benchmarked electric motors for should-cost analysis at Bosch, and ran modal and harmonic FEA at IIT Bombay. I'm now training in aerospace stress methods with MSC Nastran and Patran, including buckling and composite laminates.

CASE 01 · ACE DESIGNERS

Counterbalance cylinder elimination

₹58,000 / machine

The vertical axis of the twin spindle VMC used a counterbalance cylinder to carry the head's weight. I redesigned and validated the drive system so the axis no longer needs it.

  1. Recalculated load, inertia and torque on the vertical axis
  2. Re-sized the ball screw and servo drive to carry the head directly
  3. Validated and released for Gemini Excel and Gemini Mini Excel

CASE 02 · ACE DESIGNERS

Headstock lightweighting with FEA

−70 kg · ₹6,000 / machine

On the Gemini Junior Excel I ran a static structural analysis of the whole machine to find where the headstock actually carries load. Material outside those load paths was doing nothing, so it came out.

  1. Static structural FEA of the full machine assembly
  2. Mapped load paths and low-stress regions in the headstock casting
  3. Removed non-functional material while holding stiffness targets

Electric motor cost benchmarking

MAHLE vs Mingyang
